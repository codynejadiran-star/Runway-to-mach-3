    How to fly

    Touch: push the THRUST lever up. Pull the stick back to raise the nose and sideways to bank. A banked plane keeps turning until you level the wings, just like a real one. Drag anywhere on the sky to look around. Tap GEAR and FLAPS like a real pilot.

    Keyboard: R/F thrust · S↓ pull · W↑ push · AD bank · QE rudder · G gear · V flaps · Space brake · C camera · M map · T chat · mouse wheel zooms.
